--- interpretation and limits ---
Only the three requested `coordinate_spaces_render_wait` cases ran, with Criterion's group-configured 30 samples per case. These are end-to-end `render_scene_and_wait` timings. The reported intervals were identity 1.8264–2.1281 ms (slope estimate 1.9462 ms), shared pan/zoom 1.9726–2.1600 ms (slope estimate 2.0598 ms), and sheared rounded borders 4.7701–6.2084 ms (slope estimate 5.4812 ms). Identity had 3/30 outliers. Criterion printed change estimates for the first two cases based on any locally stored baseline; its provenance/source revision was not established here, so those comparisons are not interpreted as regressions or wins. The broad sheared interval and shared desktop/GPU environment limit precision. These measurements establish representative final-run costs only; they do not support a GPU performance-win claim or a before/after conclusion.

--- estimator verification and post-run metadata ---
Criterion's new/estimates.json files confirm that the printed time estimates above are regression slopes, with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals, not sample medians. The sample median point estimates are identity 1.867844 ms, shared pan/zoom 2.003596 ms, and sheared rounded borders 4.380829 ms. Full estimate objects are preserved in affine-aa-gpu-final-estimates.json.
