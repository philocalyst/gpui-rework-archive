# Playback occurrence continuity

The controller now names an actual playing occurrence with `PlanStamp`. Queue and policy edits that keep the current entry preserve its stamp and reconcile only changed lookahead. An EOS already in transit resolves its successor from the latest authoritative queue and availability. Explicit replay, repeat and a changed current entry install a fresh stamp; stop removes authority, and undo cannot revive a retired occurrence. Equal stamps never mean equal lookahead.

This removes the future decoder's need for a separate restart flag or reopening on an unrelated edit. It also preserves unchanged current/lookahead leases rather than cloning them during every refresh. No field, public method or dependency is added. Counter exhaustion keeps its conservative preflight and stop remains available.
