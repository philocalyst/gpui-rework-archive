    Borrow the complete replacement range before walking GPU words
    
    The first linked assembly comparison exposed two bounds checks and scalar
    ABI fragments in the two-word specialization. Borrow the whole admitted
    range once and zip its mutable words with the borrowed replacement array.
    This expresses disjoint, exactly bounded storage directly to the compiler
    and keeps the same bitwise comparison and change-envelope semantics.
    
    All eleven existing library cases pass, including all 4,096 dirty masks.
    No SIMD, timing or code-size win is assumed from this source formulation;
    the matched linked artifact must establish what the compiler actually does.
