    Skip tile coverage proofs whose result cannot change classification
    
    Hoist solid/opaque material classification out of the per-tile visit. Once
    a tile already requires samples, a translucent draw cannot restore constant
    coverage or retire its prefix, so computing its full geometric certificate
    has no observable result. Only an opaque reset candidate needs that proof.
    
    This preserves count, cutoff and constant-tag transitions exactly while
    avoiding repeated material tests and ineligible geometric work. The full
    11-library/4-presentation/52-readback default suite and doc case pass. No
    timing claim is made; the 67510c shader and its 578 exact historical captures
    remain unchanged, with this separately pinned CPU admission simplification.
