# Certified integer viewport scroll copy

Registered before implementation. Baseline4afe067fe3e243fac40369301321f44962aa808d
locally composes controlled-list dossier954ccd59a44 with native keyed workload
dossier19e47ef37f. Its only integration difference is the qualified example plus

local/gpu-scroll-copy worktree and target, all changes local. No user agent,
remote publication, physical GPU or competitor victory is implied.

Hypothesis: a proven integer scroll can reuse previously composited viewport
pixels and composite exposed strips plus other changed support. This eliminates
repeated pixel work while retaining canonical complete commands, geometry and
input/accessibility publication. It is distinct from rejected GPU text tile
threshold variants; no retuning their4096-pixel gate or accepting their failed
controls. BBTree's relevant transfer is gathering immutable evidence before an
ordered mutation, then invalidating it at structural/epoch boundaries. It is not
an external-task no-op-waker executor or a cache-hit-rate claim.

First admission domain: one vertically scrolling viewport, integral device bounds
and nonzero integral delta smaller than its height, unchanged size/profile/clear.
Require exact old/new ordered contribution equivalence over the copied overlap.
Accept only demonstrably exact raster translations: axis-aligned identity maps
with bounded integer translation, rectangular integer clips and supported solid/
immutable-mask materials. Normalize solid coverage to clipped device rectangles;
match mask resource identity/content, local domain, color, clip and translated
inverse exactly. Local glyph geometry may be fractional only when its mapped
sample coordinates are identical in both CPU-f64 and GPU-f32 arithmetic. Conservative
bounds alone are not a proof. Unsupported transforms, clip chains, gradients,
images, changed overlap, changed resources/colors/order or ambiguous arithmetic
must fall back to complete existing damage rendering. No epsilon equivalence.

Canvas staging sees both complete command banks before replacing the predecessor.
Proof must be closed and bound to that transition. Runtime may propose a viewport
from acknowledged geometry; it must not trust a proposed offset as proof of pixels.
Bind native reuse to exact presentation tokens, not source epochs alone: an
uncontrolled scroll can change the scene without advancing its source epoch.
Pending/coalesced candidates, stale/foreign tokens, failure, resize, first frame,
suspend/resume and renderer replacement must invalidate or safely fall back.
Never retain borrowed model/scene pointers or hold a mutable owner across await.

GPU copy must obey non-overlapping resource rules. Proposed implementation copies
old overlap to a separately owned scratch texture, then scratch into the destination
of the retained backing, before compositing repair rectangles and presenting the
complete image. Charge both copies, scratch growth/retention and GPU completion.
No same-texture overlapping copy. Allocate scratch only on admitted copy; ordinary
frames retain current one-image behavior. Subtract the certified destination from
ordinary conservative damage into at most four rectangles; do not collapse repairs
back into a bounding box that repaints the entire copied viewport. Reuse the same
shader and compositor for repairs. Preserve the canonical full-render oracle.

Correctness before timing: exact full-render CPU and GPU comparisons for scroll
both directions, repeated/large/end deltas, stream+scroll, clipped glyphs, alpha,
backgrounds, overlap/order/resource edits, fractional/scale/transformed fallbacks,
resize, failures and stale/coalesced token transitions. Mutants must falsify both
the content certificate and predecessor-token guard. Ordinary public production
entry points, native ACK/identity/a11y and retained-scene lifetime gates still apply.
Full GPUI/Canvas and GPU suites, native scripts, strict Clippy, locked offline
toolchain/build fingerprints and all-input freeze guards. Preserve every failure.

Before measuring, freeze concrete traces/process order and a20% minimum complete
scroll and mixed-work gain, with no control median regression above5%; include
idle, no-scroll text, small flat/full-damage and failed-admission cases. Report
p50/p95/p99, paired uncertainty, construction/cold/warm costs, source/ELF/function/
stack, all retained/transient CPU/GPU capacities and complete GPU completion, not
encoding alone. No timing beside builds/tests/allocation probes. A micro win only
admits an end-to-end trial; code-generation/fallback costs count in full.

The native fixed14px timer trace is matched; default interactive trajectories are
not: pinned ZGX uses platform wheel conversion (Linux default40) and resampling,
GPUI32 and direct events. Earlier wheel28 notes were wrong. Deferred controls,
native retained-count observability, matched release profiles, cross-framework text
quality and two physical hardware classes remain independent gates. No broad ZGX
ranking until those applicable workload and hardware requirements are met.
