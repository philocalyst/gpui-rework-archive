Close preemption proof: exact source Loom case holds the producer's mutex and
waits for a resume channel. The owner calls atomic close then sends resume.
Any locking close would introduce a Loom-detected wait cycle; current close
returns. A second case verifies a remaining physical lease defers one payload
until its Arc drops, with no post-close admission. No timed sleep is used.
Publication wake proof and old-notification/lease-reuse cases use actual std/
Loom-shared code, not a separately rewritten channel abstraction. Standard
tests cover a payload destructor panic outside the mutex; public tests cover
staged response drop on earlier model-callback panic and ready+late-cap close.
